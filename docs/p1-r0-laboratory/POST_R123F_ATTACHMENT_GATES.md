# P1-R0 post-R123F attachment gates

Status: design-only preparation. This document does not authorize production attachment, database migration, route creation, revision advancement, or service restart.

## Gate 0: benchmark closure

- Preserve the R123F V3 ledger and milestone files exactly.
- Require either an unqualified V3 72-hour PASS or a separately reviewed V4 PASS with the V3 result still visible.
- Require one collector, zero restarts, stable BSF/SNTSS/Chronobiology/fetus identity, advancing checkpoints, zero SNTSS output, zero resident authority, no abandonment, and SQLite quick-check success.
- A V4 `EVIDENCE_INCOMPLETE` or `OBSERVED_FAILURES` blocks attachment.

## Gate 1: exact source and artifact fence

- Start from the exact merged R123F production commit and tree. Do not merge laboratory runtime code into the already benchmarked release.
- Re-run focused regressions, the complete suite, clean extracted-archive validation, every-file inventory/hash verification, shell/JavaScript syntax, protected anchors, and real Bubblewrap/server entry paths.
- Bind controller, release, tree, manifest, forward, recovery, and archive SHA-256 identities before host preflight.
- Keep the public Certification Lab as test infrastructure only; immutable release identity remains Git-bound and independently verified.

## Gate 2: non-mutating production preflight

- Prove the exact current release pointer, R123F frozen revision, stable PID/restart counters, database quick-check, and benchmark closure hashes.
- Prove `BSF · LIVE`, `SNTSS · SHADOW`, and `CHRONOBIOLOGY · SHADOW` with their existing identities.
- Prove SNTSS zero output/authority, Chronobiology continuity, healthy fetus continuity, and unchanged cgroup/deadline/resource contracts.
- Preview every additive StateStore migration against a clone and prove existing biological rows are byte-equivalent after rollback rehearsal.

## Gate 3: revision-fenced METAB birth

- Advance only to the next explicitly owner-authorized revision; never invent or reuse an identity.
- Commit the METAB founder exactly once with parent/hash/generation fencing.
- Attach METAB first in `NEUTRAL`, with zero biological output permission and no route to SNTSS, HOMEOS, INTERO, or production effectors.
- Show a persistent `METAB · NEUTRAL` lifecycle chip only after accepted residency. Before acceptance it remains the non-live `METAB · LAB QUALIFIED` roadmap label.
- On failure before durable revision advancement, allow exact pointer rollback. After advancement, preserve state and use forward recovery only.

## Gate 4: METAB shadow qualification

- Admit only committed, trusted future-frame inputs through manifested BSF routes.
- Prove deterministic replay, checkpoint continuity, bounded queues, unchanged resource limits, no acquired-burden normalization, and no authority.
- Promote METAB to `SHADOW` only after real execution and zero-authority route containment are proven. `SHADOW` must never mean merely installed.

## Gate 5: HOMEOS birth and shadow qualification

- Require committed METAB summaries from strictly earlier frames; direct source reads and same-frame feedback remain forbidden.
- Attach HOMEOS in `NEUTRAL` with zero output permission, then qualify it separately for `SHADOW`.
- Preserve the METAB identity and checkpoint lineage throughout. HOMEOS failure cannot rewrite or rewind METAB history.
- The public rail changes only HOMEOS from roadmap label to persistent lifecycle chip after accepted residency.

## Gate 6: INTERO birth and shadow qualification

- Require delayed committed METAB/HOMEOS summaries under the frozen 2/3/2 founder delays.
- Keep INTERO perception-only: no emotion, diagnosis, cause, self, action, organ target, or authority semantics.
- Attach in `NEUTRAL`, qualify independently for `SHADOW`, and preserve upstream ancestry and replay evidence.

## Gate 7: bounded SNTSS receptor

- Keep the SNTSS interoceptive receptor route physically absent through every LAB and NEUTRAL stage.
- Add exactly one bounded, revocable receptor only under a separate owner-authorized route tranche.
- Prove receptor revocation/removal, zero SNTSS production output, no authority grant, no direct HOMEOS/METAB reads, and no route widening.
- Receptor failure or removal cannot erase INTERO, HOMEOS, or METAB founder/checkpoint/chip history.

## Gate 8: web-chip and rollback acceptance

- Preserve fixed rail order: BSF, SNTSS, CHRONOBIOLOGY, METAB, HOMEOS, INTERO.
- Roadmap labels remain visibly non-live and have no health dot, checkpoint, or biological state.
- Born chips persist through NEUTRAL, SHADOW, DEGRADED, RECOVERING, QUARANTINED, and OFFLINE.
- The projection remains observation-only with no attach, mode, authority, route, or checkpoint mutation endpoint.
- Rehearse pre-advance pointer rollback and post-advance forward recovery from a cloned production-shaped StateStore before any live execution.

## Stop conditions

Stop without attachment if any gate shows a changed R123F identity, benchmark evidence mismatch, collector restart, failed or persistent delivery, SNTSS output, resident authority, fetus discontinuity, route widening, resource-contract change, database mutation during preflight, missing rollback/forward-recovery proof, or incomplete owner authorization.
