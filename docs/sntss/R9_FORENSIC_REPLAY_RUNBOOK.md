# SNTSS R9 Forensic Replay Runbook

## Inputs

A reviewer receives the retained forensic bundle, the trusted initial checkpoint/audit anchor, expected candidate version, expected profile hash and—when available—the externally retained expected chain head/count.

No raw private stimulus payload is required for transition explanation.

## Verification order

1. Verify every segment-manifest canonical hash.
2. Verify segment indices, sequence ranges and anchor-to-head continuity from the trusted initial anchor.
3. Verify the current retained segment starts from the most recent segment head.
4. Recompute every retained record hash from canonical JSON.
5. Require strict sequence continuity and `previousRecordHash` continuity.
6. Compare candidate version and profile hash with the pinned evidence index.
7. Compare total record count and final head hash when an external head/count is available.
8. Only after chain verification, select the unique transition ID and produce the deterministic explanation object.

## Failure interpretation

- `SNTSS_FORENSIC_TAMPER`: record content changed after hashing.
- `SNTSS_FORENSIC_CHAIN`: anchor/previous-head continuity broke.
- `SNTSS_FORENSIC_SEQUENCE`: record or segment omission/reorder/gap.
- `SNTSS_FORENSIC_HEAD`: final head differs from the trusted evidence index.
- `SNTSS_FORENSIC_COUNT`: truncation or count mismatch.
- `SNTSS_FORENSIC_CANDIDATE`: evidence was generated by a different candidate.
- `SNTSS_FORENSIC_PROFILE`: evidence used a different calibration profile.
- `SNTSS_FORENSIC_EXPLAIN`: transition is absent or ambiguous in retained evidence.

A replay failure invalidates the explanation claim. It does not authorize chemistry mutation or recovery by itself.
