# SNTSS R8 Quarantine and Neutralization Runbook

## Trigger

Use this procedure only for a trusted Kernel incident record. Never inject destructive faults into the active state path. If containment of the Kernel, active fetus or StateStore is uncertain, stop the candidate drill and preserve the entire disposable lab directory.

## Automatic sequence

1. Stop accepting new SNTSS stimuli and open the narrowest receptor/source/transmitter circuit available.
2. Record the incident ID, kind, scope, cursor, evidence hash, last verified checkpoint hash and detail hash in the bounded containment chain.
3. On a critical or repeated event, quarantine SNTSS. Do not alter acquired transmitter, receptor, habituation, lease or developmental state.
4. Produce a neutral-degradation directive: expire derived leases, emit no chemistry frames, preserve the failed state, and leave organism identity and StateStore authority with the Kernel.
5. For process escape, governor bypass, invalid checkpoint or unsafe shutdown, the Kernel sends `SIGKILL`. SNTSS itself has no termination authority.
6. Retain the failed state/checkpoint/evidence references. Any restart is shadow-only and must load a hash-verified matching checkpoint. No verified checkpoint means remain failed; fresh chemistry is forbidden.

## Operator checks

- Confirm Kernel identity is unchanged and Kernel health is `ok`.
- Confirm StateStore integrity, heartbeat and write status are healthy.
- Confirm the active fetus PID/lifecycle and authority epoch did not change because of the SNTSS incident.
- Confirm the candidate cgroup reports the expected `memory.high`, `memory.max`, `pids.max` and `cpu.max` values.
- Confirm SNTSS has zero declared outputs and no environment, child-process, filesystem-write, network or inspector capability.
- Archive cgroup events, exit signal, resource samples, containment evidence head, failed-state hash and checkpoint hash.

## Recovery decision

Recovery may proceed only in shadow mode after package hash, profile hash, identity, lineage, checkpoint and incident-chain verification. A probe may recommend continued shadowing or rejection; it may not write chemistry. Repeated failure, any evidence break, a resource-governor bypass or any foundation degradation is an automatic R8 blocker.

## Escalation

Do not edit a checkpoint, delete failed state, author replacement chemistry or re-enable circuits manually. Preserve evidence and return the candidate to engineering review. Deployment, live installation and promotion require later roadmap gates and explicit operator authorization.
